## 👨‍🔬 About Me

> *"I treat biological systems as dynamical systems first, and develop code guided by physical intuition alongside computational rigor to make their hidden structure visible."*

I'm a **BSMS Physics Student at IISER Pune** working at the intersection of nonlinear dynamics, computational biology, and machine learning. My research centres on building numerical simulations of complex physical systems — from Turing pattern formation in reaction–diffusion PDEs to cancer cell-state transitions via gene-regulatory circuits. I have hands-on experience translating continuous mathematical models (ODEs, PDEs, RMT) into reproducible, well-documented Python pipelines that generate both rigorous scientific insight and publication-ready visualisations.

- 🔬 **Research focus:** Reaction–Diffusion systems · Epithelial–Mesenchymal Transition (EMT) · Nonlinear dynamics
- 🧠 **Methods:** Bifurcation analysis · Basin-of-attraction mapping · Random Matrix Theory · Transfer Learning
- 🛠️ **Stack:** Python · NumPy · SciPy · Numba · PyTorch · Matplotlib · Jupyter
- 🏛️ **Supervised by:** Ushasi Roy, IISER Pune (Computational Biophysics group)
- 🎯 **Currently:** Building spatially-extended models of cancer metastasis mechanics
- 📖 **Open to:** Research internships · Ph.D. positions · Computational Physics/Biology roles · Industry Job positions

### 🌀 Reaction–Diffusion Systems as Data-Generating Dynamical Systems
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-View_Repository-58a6ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/vajadiye-gif/Reaction-Diffusion-Systems-as-Data-Generating-Dynamical-Systems)

![Jupyter](https://img.shields.io/badge/Jupyter-F37626?style=flat-square&logo=jupyter&logoColor=white)

> **Simulating three canonical nonlinear models — Gray–Scott, Multi-fate, and EMT — to study how PDE dynamics generate complex spatiotemporal pattern datasets.**

**What I built:**
- Full finite-difference solvers with explicit Euler integration and periodic/Neumann boundary conditions for three distinct reaction–diffusion systems
- Interactive `ipywidgets` parameter dashboards enabling real-time exploration of feed/kill rates ($F$, $k$) and diffusion coefficients
- Spatiotemporal analysis identifying how parameter choices drive transitions between labyrinths, spots, spirals, and steady states
- Exported simulation datasets (images, GIFs, `.npy` arrays) for downstream ML use

**Key techniques:** PDE numerics · Turing instability · Activator-inhibitor dynamics · Parameter sensitivity studies

### 🧫 EMT Computational Biophysics — Gene Regulatory Circuit Analysis
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-View_Repository-58a6ff?style=for-the-badge&logo=github)](https://github.com/vajadiye-gif/EMT-Circuit-Analysis)
![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3776AB?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![Numba](https://img.shields.io/badge/Numba-JIT%20Compiled-00a3e0?style=flat-square&logo=python&logoColor=white)
![SciPy](https://img.shields.io/badge/SciPy-8CAAE6?style=flat-square&logo=scipy&logoColor=white)

> **End-to-end computational study of cancer metastasis mechanics: from single-cell ODE tristability to spatially-extended 2D simulation and Random Matrix Theory spectral analysis.**

**What I built:**
- **7-variable Numba-JIT ODE** encoding combinatorial miRNA–mRNA binding for the SNAIL/ZEB/miR-200/miR-34 circuit — the core regulatory network of cancer metastasis
- **Bifurcation analysis** revealing tristability (Epithelial, Hybrid E/M, Mesenchymal states) via quasi-static hysteresis sweeps over the TGF-β proxy signal
- **Basin-of-attraction mapping** with 105 perturbation trajectories demonstrating that the Mesenchymal state has the widest basin — the most difficult to reverse therapeutically
- **2D operator-splitting PDE solver** (Lie-Trotter, Numba-parallel on 50×50 grid) showing that diffusion destroys the Hybrid branch, leaving only E and M — consistent with experimental findings
- **Random Matrix Theory analysis** treating the ZEB concentration field as a random matrix: the emergence of a rank-1 outlier eigenvalue signals long-range spatial order as M-patches expand

**Supervised under:** Ushasi Roy, IISER Pune · *Semester project in computational biophysics, 2025–2026*

**Key techniques:** Bifurcation theory · Basin-of-attraction analysis · Operator-splitting PDE numerics · Numba parallelisation · Random Matrix Theory
